Source SVGs are preserved; PNGs are rasterized copies for figure rendering.

The five `fig02_*.svg` / `fig02_*.png` input icons are exported from the paper's
existing programmatic glyphs in `figure_palette.py` and the force-history
illustration in `draw_fig02_architecture.py`. Regenerate these icons using
`export_fig02_icons.py` in this directory.

The `fig01_camera`, `fig01_instruction`, `fig01_teacher`, and `fig01_student`
files retain the Twemoji artwork and license stated above. The other `fig01_*`
icons are the paper's original vector elements extracted from
`fig01_method_overview.svg`, preserving their geometry, colors, and strokes.
Regenerate these assets with `export_fig01_icons.py` after verifying its source
SVG revision. PNG versions have transparent backgrounds and 512-pixel sides.
