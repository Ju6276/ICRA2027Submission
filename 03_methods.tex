\subsection{Problem Formulation}
\label{sec:problem}

We study contact-rich manipulation in which a policy observes images $V$, a language instruction $L$, robot state $S$, and a timestamped stream of force/torque measurements $F$. Force is available during both training and deployment and updates independently of visual--language inference. We start from a pretrained force-conditioned VLA teacher $T_\theta$, which is frozen throughout.

Actions are expressed in the end-effector frame as
\begin{equation}
    A = [\, p \in \mathbb{R}^{3};\; r \in \mathbb{R}^{6};\; g \in \mathbb{R} \,] \in \mathbb{R}^{10},
    \label{eq:action_space}
\end{equation}
where $r$ is the continuous 6D rotation representation~\cite{zhou2019continuity}, $g$ is the gripper command, and $A^{p}=[p;r]\in\mathbb{R}^{9}$ is the pose component.

Let $t_k$ be the observation timestamp of the most recent slow update and $t$ the current control instant. We decompose the command into a reference and a correction,
\begin{equation}
    A^{\mathrm{cmd}}(t)
    =
    A^{\mathrm{ref}}(t)
    +
    \Delta A(t),
    \qquad
    \Delta A(t) \in \mathbb{R}^{9},
    \label{eq:action_comp}
\end{equation}
where $A^{\mathrm{ref}}(t)$ is interpolated from the action chunk generated at $t_k$, and the correction acts on pose alone; grasp transitions are semantic rather than reactive, so the gripper channel remains with the slow branch.

The value of Eq.~\eqref{eq:action_comp} is that the two terms have different costs. The reference varies on the semantic timescale and is obtained by interpolating a cached chunk, so a new force measurement requires recomputing only $\Delta A$. The difficulty is that the decomposition is not identified by the data: demonstrations supervise the executed action, and any $(A^{\mathrm{ref}}, \Delta A)$ summing to it is equally consistent with behavior cloning. Nothing prevents the reference from absorbing contact-dependent behavior while the correction reproduces nominal motion, in which case the reactive branch is as expensive as the policy it was meant to relieve. We therefore construct an explicit target for $\Delta A$ from the frozen teacher.

\subsection{Counterfactual Residual Targets}
\label{sec:teacher}

Our target is the change in the teacher's output caused by conditioning on the measured force rather than on no force. Obtaining it requires only two queries of a frozen model, but for their difference to be usable as supervision, three properties must hold. The counterfactual branch must remain a competent policy, or the difference measures degradation instead of force. The force condition must be the only quantity that differs between the queries, or the difference is dominated by other variation. And the target must depend only on information the student can observe when it acts. We establish these in turn.

\subsubsection{Force-conditioned teacher.}

The teacher follows ForceVLA~\cite{yu2025forcevla}, a $\pi_0$-style flow-matching policy~\cite{black2024pi0}, with its instantaneous force input replaced by a causal temporal encoder. At each teacher inference time $t_k$ we read a fixed-duration window from the timestamped native-rate stream,
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{F}_k
    =
    \left\{
        F(\tau)
        \mid
        t_k-w \leq \tau \leq t_k
    \right\},
    \label{eq:force_window}
\end{equation}
rather than sampling force at the visual observation rate, so that contact onset, impact transients, and recovery fall inside the conditioning signal. A validity mask separates an unfilled window from one measuring genuine zero force. A causal temporal convolutional network encodes the window into a single token,
\begin{equation}
    z_k^F
    =
    E_T^F(\mathcal{F}_k),
    \label{eq:teacher_tcn}
\end{equation}
which enters the existing fusion module in place of the instantaneous force embedding, leaving the action-generation pathway unchanged. The factual prediction is
\begin{equation}
    A_{T,k}^{\mathrm{full}}
    =
    T_{\theta}\!\left(V_k,L,S_k,z_k^F\right)
    \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times 10}.
    \label{eq:full_teacher}
\end{equation}

\subsubsection{A competent counterfactual.}
\label{sec:null_decomp}

To query the same teacher without the current measurement, we replace $z_k^F$ with a learnable token $z_{\varnothing F} \in \mathbb{R}^{D_F}$. Because it is shared across samples, it represents the absence of an instance-specific force observation rather than a physical zero: it can carry a dataset-level prior, but not the current force history. This distinguishes the query from a zero-padded input, which is out of distribution for a network trained on real force and whose output change therefore conflates the effect of force with the response to an invalid input.

Substituting the token alone still leaves the policy degraded, since the teacher was trained with force available. We therefore let the counterfactual branch recover. Holding all teacher weights frozen, we optimize $z_{\varnothing F}$ together with a rank-$\rho$ adapter that corrects the pose channels of the flow velocity,
\begin{equation}
    v^{p} \leftarrow v^{p} + W_{\mathrm{out}}\,\sigma\!\left(W_{\mathrm{in}}\,h\right),
    \quad
    W_{\mathrm{in}} \in \mathbb{R}^{\rho \times d},\;
    W_{\mathrm{out}} \in \mathbb{R}^{9 \times \rho},
    \label{eq:nominal_adapter}
\end{equation}
with $h$ the action-expert hidden state and $\sigma$ the SiLU nonlinearity, under the teacher's action regression loss,
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{L}_{\varnothing}
    =
    d\!\left(
        T_{\theta}(V_k,L,S_k,z_{\varnothing F}),\;
        A_k^{\mathrm{expert}}
    \right).
    \label{eq:null_training}
\end{equation}
The adapter is active only when $z_{\varnothing F}$ is supplied, so the factual branch of Eq.~\eqref{eq:full_teacher} is bitwise unchanged and the comparison is not confounded by additional capacity given to both sides. Eq.~\eqref{eq:null_training} therefore produces an adapted null-force \emph{mode} of one network, not a second policy; the same mode serves as the slow branch at deployment, so no nominal policy is distilled.

\subsubsection{Isolating the force condition.}
\label{sec:teacher_differencing}

The teacher is a stochastic sampler, so two independent queries would differ by the sampling noise as well as by the force condition. We remove every other source of variation: the vision--language prefix and its key/value cache are computed once and reused, and a single draw $\epsilon$ initializes both flow integrations,
\begin{align}
    A_{T,k}^{\mathrm{full}}
    &=
    T_{\theta}\!\left(V_k,L,S_k,z_k^F;\,\epsilon\right),
    \label{eq:teacher_full_again}
    \\
    A_{T,k}^{\mathrm{null}}
    &=
    T_{\theta}\!\left(V_k,L,S_k,z_{\varnothing F};\,\epsilon\right),
    \quad
    \epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0,I).
    \label{eq:teacher_null}
\end{align}
The draw is keyed deterministically to the sample index rather than to sampler state, so the pairing holds even when the two branches are evaluated in separate passes over the dataset. Sec.~\ref{sec:exp_ablation} reports what happens when $\epsilon$ is drawn independently. Reusing the prefix additionally halves the cost of extraction, the vision--language pass being its dominant term.

The counterfactual prediction is the reference,
\begin{equation}
    A_{T,k}^{\mathrm{ref}}
    =
    A_{T,k}^{\mathrm{null}},
    \label{eq:nominal_target}
\end{equation}
and the \emph{counterfactual residual} is their difference on the pose channels,
\begin{equation}
    \Delta A_{T,k}^{\mathrm{cf}}
    =
    \left(A_{T,k}^{\mathrm{full}}\right)^{p}
    -
    \left(A_{T,k}^{\mathrm{null}}\right)^{p}
    \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times 9}.
    \label{eq:teacher_residual}
\end{equation}
Two properties of Eq.~\eqref{eq:teacher_residual} bear on how it should be read. It is defined at the level of the model rather than the environment: the null branch carries its own adapter, and vision and state condition both queries and may themselves reveal contact, so the residual is the teacher's complete mode change under the substitution, not a uniquely attributable force effect. It is also causal with respect to the student's information, because the teacher produced the entire chunk from the single observation at $t_k$; step $j$ of the target is the teacher's response to force already observed, and no future measurement enters the label.

\subsection{Residual Policy}
\label{sec:residual_policy}

\subsubsection{What the reactive branch observes.}
\label{sec:slow}

Each slow update publishes the reference chunk $A_{T,k}^{\mathrm{null}}$ and, from the same forward pass, a compact summary of the task context. The vision--language prefix $H_k \in \mathbb{R}^{S \times d}$ spans several hundred tokens and is reduced by masked mean pooling over $M$ contiguous bins,
\begin{equation}
    Z_k^{\mathrm{intent}}
    =
    \mathrm{Pool}_M\!\left(H_k, m_k\right)
    \in \mathbb{R}^{M \times d}.
    \label{eq:intent_projection}
\end{equation}
Pooling is fixed rather than learned, so the same summary is reproducible at deployment from the live prefix without additional parameters; a learned projector inside the student maps the bins to its own width.

The reactive branch therefore reads force at the sensor rate, but reads vision only through $Z_k^{\mathrm{intent}}$, which is as old as the last slow update. This asymmetry is deliberate and follows from Eq.~\eqref{eq:teacher_residual}: the prefix is held fixed across the paired queries, so the target contains no variation that a fresher image could explain. Supplying a new image to the student would not reduce its loss, while adding a visual encoder to the reactive path would remove the cost advantage that motivates the decomposition. The student is instead told how stale its context is, through the time feature of Sec.~\ref{sec:rate_agnostic}.

\subsubsection{Architecture.}
\label{sec:fast}

The student maps force history and cached context to a short chunk of pose corrections in one deterministic forward pass. A causal TCN of the same family as the teacher's encodes the native-rate window $\mathcal{F}_t$ into $Z_t^F$, with the validity mask applied at its input. The remaining inputs are an unordered set of conditions, each projected to a common width and tagged with a learned type embedding,
\begin{equation}
    X_t
    =
    \left[
        \widetilde{Z}_k^{\mathrm{intent}};\;
        Z_t^F;\;
        P_S(S_t);\;
        P_A(A^{\mathrm{ref}}(t));\;
        P_\Gamma(\gamma_t);\;
        q
    \right],
    \label{eq:fast_token_sequence}
\end{equation}
where $\gamma_t$ is the time feature and $q$ a learned query. No positional encoding is used, as the conditions have no meaningful order. A single pre-norm block with grouped-query attention processes the set, the conditions attending to one another while $q$ reads them without being visible in return, and a linear head maps the query state to the chunk,
\begin{equation}
    \Delta \hat{A}_{t,1:K}
    =
    W_{\mathrm{res}}\,H_t[q]
    \in \mathbb{R}^{K \times 9}.
    \label{eq:residual_output}
\end{equation}
Zero-initializing $W_{\mathrm{res}}$ makes the untrained student an identity controller, so training departs from $A^{\mathrm{cmd}} = A^{\mathrm{ref}}$ rather than from a random perturbation of it.

Emitting $K$ steps spaced by the action period $\tau_A$, rather than a single correction, decouples the student's update rate from the actuator's. If an update is late, or the actuator runs faster than the student, later steps of the previous chunk are still valid commands for the instants they were predicted for.

\subsubsection{Objective.}
\label{sec:loss}

Only the student is trained. With $w_j \propto \beta^{\,j-1}$ normalized weights over the leading $K$ steps of the teacher targets, one term supervises the correction directly,
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{res}}
    =
    \sum_{j=1}^{K} w_j \,
    d\!\left(
        \Delta \hat{A}_{t,j},\;
        \Delta A^{\mathrm{cf}}_{T,j}
    \right),
    \label{eq:res_loss}
\end{equation}
and the other requires the composed command to reproduce the factual teacher,
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{comp}}
    =
    \sum_{j=1}^{K} w_j \,
    d\!\left(
        \left(A^{\mathrm{ref}}_j\right)^{p} + \Delta \hat{A}_{t,j},\;
        \left(A^{\mathrm{full}}_{T,j}\right)^{p}
    \right),
    \label{eq:comp_loss}
\end{equation}
giving
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{L}
    =
    \lambda_{\mathrm{res}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{res}}
    +
    \lambda_{\mathrm{comp}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{comp}}.
    \label{eq:total_loss}
\end{equation}
The two terms differ in what they constrain: Eq.~\eqref{eq:res_loss} keeps the student specialized to the correction, while Eq.~\eqref{eq:comp_loss} penalizes errors that survive composition with the reference the student will actually ride. The decay $\beta<1$ weights the first step, which executes whenever the student keeps up, above the later steps, which execute only when it does not. No reference-imitation term is needed, since the reference comes from the frozen teacher, and no behavior-cloning term is applied, since regressing the student onto demonstrated actions would reintroduce the total action it was constructed to exclude.

\subsection{Timing-Randomized Training and Asynchronous Execution}
\label{sec:rate_agnostic}

The reference age depends on the slow policy's rate and inference latency, neither of which is fixed in practice. A student trained at one operating point learns that point, so we treat timing as a distribution rather than a setting. Targets are extracted once at the full observation rate, and every $N_\tau$ training steps we redraw a slow update period from $[1/f_{\max}, 1/f_{\min}]$, an inference latency $\ell$ from $[\ell_{\min},\ell_{\max}]$, and per-update jitter from $[-\eta,\eta]$, then reassign packets to control instants. A packet published at $t_k$ becomes usable only at $t_k+\ell$, control instants before that are excluded, and a packet overtaken in flight by a newer one is discarded. Since the student never sees a zero-age context, it is never trained on a condition unattainable on hardware.

Rather than infer the operating point, the student is told it,
\begin{equation}
    \gamma_t
    =
    \left[
        \frac{t - t_k}{c},\;
        \alpha_t
    \right],
    \qquad
    \alpha_t = \frac{t - t_j}{\tau_A} \in [0,1),
    \label{eq:time_features}
\end{equation}
where $c$ is a fixed scale covering the randomized band and $t_j$ the chunk waypoint preceding $t$. The two features are not redundant: the first accumulates across a slow period and reports how stale the semantic context is, while the second is periodic within it and locates the command inside an action interval. Algorithm~\ref{alg:pipeline} collects the resulting procedure.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{Counterfactual residual distillation}
\label{alg:pipeline}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\REQUIRE demonstrations $\mathcal{D}$, frozen teacher $T_\theta$, chunk length $K$
\STATE optimize $z_{\varnothing F}$, $(W_{\mathrm{in}},W_{\mathrm{out}})$ by Eq.~\eqref{eq:null_training}
\FOR{each observation $k$ in $\mathcal{D}$}
\STATE compute prefix $H_k$ and its key/value cache; key $\epsilon_k\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I)$ to $k$
\STATE $A^{\mathrm{full}}_{T,k}\!\leftarrow\!T_\theta(\cdot,z_k^F;\epsilon_k)$, \quad $A^{\mathrm{null}}_{T,k}\!\leftarrow\!T_\theta(\cdot,z_{\varnothing F};\epsilon_k)$
\STATE $Z_k^{\mathrm{intent}}\!\leftarrow\!\mathrm{Pool}_M(H_k,m_k)$; \; $\Delta A^{\mathrm{cf}}_{T,k}$ by Eq.~\eqref{eq:teacher_residual}
\STATE store $(A^{\mathrm{null}}_{T,k},\,\Delta A^{\mathrm{cf}}_{T,k},\,Z_k^{\mathrm{intent}},\,t_k)$
\ENDFOR
\FOR{each training step}
\STATE every $N_\tau$ steps: redraw slow period, latency $\ell$, and jitter; reassign packets
\STATE form $A^{\mathrm{ref}}(t)$, window $\mathcal{F}_t$, and $\gamma_t$ by Eq.~\eqref{eq:time_features}
\STATE minimize Eq.~\eqref{eq:total_loss} over the student
\ENDFOR
\ENSURE reactive residual student
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

At deployment three loops run concurrently. The teacher runs in null mode and publishes a packet holding the reference chunk, the pooled intent bins, the observation timestamp, and the time at which the packet became available. The student reads the newest packet, force window, and state, and publishes a command chunk stamped with the interval over which it is valid. The actuator samples that chunk at the control instant and applies Eq.~\eqref{eq:action_comp}, rebasing the reference onto the state its own packet observed rather than the current one. Separating publication from consumption keeps the actuator from blocking on either model: a late student update degrades to a later step of the previous chunk, which is the case Eq.~\eqref{eq:residual_output} is supervised for, and when no unexpired chunk remains the actuator holds instead of extrapolating.

The cost of the reactive path follows directly. Cached-prefix slow--fast policies keep an action expert in that loop and pay $N_{\mathrm{flow}}$ denoising passes over it per update, or $\mathcal{O}(N_{\mathrm{flow}} L_{\mathrm{AE}} d_{\mathrm{AE}}^{2})$ for depth $L_{\mathrm{AE}}$ and width $d_{\mathrm{AE}}$. Our update is one deterministic pass over a small network, or $\mathcal{O}(L_{S} d_{S}^{2})$, reducing the step count and the depth--width product together.
