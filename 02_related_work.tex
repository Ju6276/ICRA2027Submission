\subsection{Force-Aware and Multi-Rate VLA Control}
Force-aware VLAs incorporate measured force/torque through multimodal fusion, force prompting, or temporal force memory, while related approaches distill force representations from other observations or rebalance force learning during optimization~\cite{yu2025forcevlaenhancingvlamodels,li2026forcevla2unleashinghybridforceposition,zhang2025tavlaelucidatingdesignspace,li2026fmvlaforcebasedmemoryvisionlanguageaction,zhao2026fdvlaforcedistilledvisionlanguageactionmodel,zhang2026craftadaptingvlamodels}. Conventional force-conditioned VLA policies improve contact-sensitive action generation but typically encode nominal motion and physical adjustment within a complete action prediction.

Multi-rate policies separate the update schedules of semantic and physical computation. Visuomotor approaches learn from asynchronous RGB and force/torque streams or combine low-rate planning with a structurally separated fast pathway~\cite{lee2025manipforceforceguidedpolicylearning,chen2026implicitrdpendtoendvisualforcediffusion,wang2026phaforcephasescheduledvisualforcepolicy}. Among VLAs, FAVLA reuses cached semantic features for faster force-conditioned action inference, LIFT post-trains a force-memory-conditioned reactive expert with corrective rollouts, and DAM-VLA refreshes modality-specific latent buffers at their sensor rates~\cite{li2026favlaforceadaptivefastslowvla,wang2026lateforceacceleratingvla,vanjani2026damvladecoupledasynchronousmultimodal}. These methods separate update rates through caching, parallel experts, latent buffers, or prescribed fast-correction structures. Our focus is complementary: deriving explicit supervision for an additive correction from an offline force-aware teacher and executing it independently of full-action generation.

\subsection{Residual Policy Learning}
Residual policies combine a nominal controller with an online correction, making them well suited to physical interaction. Residual refinement methods commonly define the correction through task rewards or additional on-policy human corrections~\cite{ankile2024imitationrefinementresidual,xu2025compliantresidualdaggerimproving}, while fast-correction policies can impose a residual structure during imitation~\cite{wang2026phaforcephasescheduledvisualforcepolicy}. We instead construct an explicit correction target offline from paired predictions of a pretrained force-aware teacher.

\subsection{Conditional Distillation}
Learned missing-modality tokens and masked modality projection distinguish unavailable inputs from physically meaningful zeros and reconstruct their representations from the remaining modalities~\cite{ramazanova2024exploringmissingmodalitymultimodal,nezakati2024mmprobustmultimodallearning}. Conditional and unconditional prediction differences can define model-dependent updates that are transferred to a single predictor through guidance distillation~\cite{ho2022classifierfreediffusionguidance,meng2023distillationguideddiffusionmodels}. We use paired full-force and null-force predictions to construct explicit correction supervision for the residual policy.
