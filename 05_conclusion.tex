ForceDelta-VLA distills force and staleness residuals from cached-context teacher queries and executes them independently of the generative reference. On nine real-robot tasks it reaches an $82.2\%$ mean success rate and lowers peak contact force by about $16\%$ and $19\%$ versus ImplicitRDP on the single-arm and bimanual platforms, with the shortest mean successful-trial completion time on both. The residual meets the nominal target-update budget; the reference is refreshed asynchronously. Ablations support paired wrench history, the two heads, and schedule replay.

A pose residual cannot replace an invalid reference or do nonlocal recovery, and it assumes cached visual context stays valid within a reference interval. Robot-estimated wrench also limits sensitivity to weak contact. Future work includes confidence-triggered reference refresh, lightweight current-visual updates, richer contact sensing, and recovery from corrective rollouts.
