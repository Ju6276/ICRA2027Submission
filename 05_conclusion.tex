We presented ForceDelta-VLA, which derives force and staleness corrections from a frozen force-aware teacher and distills them into a residual policy that executes independently of full-action generation. Across nine real-robot tasks, ForceDelta-VLA achieves an $82.2\%$ mean success rate and the lowest peak contact force among the evaluated methods on both platforms.

The method inherits errors and coverage limits from its offline teacher, and its bounded residual cannot represent nonlocal strategy changes. Its sensitivity to subtle contact changes is limited by robot-estimated wrench. The current single-GPU JAX deployment limits residual throughput through contention with generative reference inference. Optimized inference or dedicated compute could increase the update rate. Future work will incorporate corrective rollouts and nonlocal recovery policies.
