We presented ForceDelta-VLA, a framework for deriving an independently executable force-responsive correction from a pretrained force-aware VLA. A learned null-force mode supplies the reference, and matched full-force and null-force predictions define explicit action-space supervision for the force-conditioned residual. A complementary staleness target trains the same compact policy to compensate for reference motion that becomes outdated during asynchronous inference. This formulation preserves complete action generation in the teacher while updating contact-dependent pose corrections from recent force history without repeatedly generating complete action chunks. \assumed{Final text will summarize the measured success, peak-force, force-impulse, and latency improvements after completing the robot evaluation.}

The method remains limited by the accuracy and coverage of its offline teacher, while its bounded additive residual cannot represent discrete strategy changes. Moreover, the paired target is a model-defined conditional difference rather than a causal attribution of force, and asynchronous composition has no formal safety guarantee under variable delay. Future work will incorporate limited on-policy correction, discrete recovery decisions, and delay-aware safety constraints.
