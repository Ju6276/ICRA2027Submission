We introduced ForceDelta-VLA, a force-aware VLA that replaces reactive full-action generation with counterfactual residual distillation. Paired factual-force and adapted null-force predictions of a frozen teacher define the residual target, while the null-mode prediction serves as the reference. A lightweight causal student predicts pose corrections from timestamped force history, cached task context, robot state, and the current reference. This decomposition turns the reactive pathway from an iterative generative policy into a deterministic residual predictor.

The counterfactual in ForceDelta-VLA is defined at the model level rather than as a causal intervention. The null query contains learned parameters, while vision and robot state retain contact cues; consequently, the resulting residual cannot be interpreted as a uniquely attributable force contribution. Offline distillation also inherits teacher errors and remains bounded by the coverage of the demonstrations. Moreover, an additive pose residual cannot represent a discrete change of manipulation strategy.

Future work will incorporate limited on-policy correction, extend the residual policy with discrete recovery decisions, and establish stability and safety guarantees for asynchronous composition under inference delay.
